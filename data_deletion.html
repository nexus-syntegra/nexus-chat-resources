Delete Your Nexus Chat Data

Nexus Chat by Nexus Syntegra. Updated October 7, 2026.

Nexus Chat has no user accounts, so there is no account to close. Your conversations, memories, documents and settings live on your phone, and you can erase them yourself at any time. A small amount of data is held off the phone; this page explains how to have that deleted too.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open Nexus Chat.
  2. Go to Settings > Security > Delete All Data.
  3. Confirm. The App erases its data, and its next launch starts as a first run.

This erases conversations, memories, imported documents, voice clones, the app suite (calendar, tasks, notes, alarms, timers, money, habits, journal and sleep), automations, settings, saved API keys and credentials, and the in-app browser's cookies and storage. It signs out of Google and Tailscale. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Downloaded AI models are kept, because they hold none of your data and are large to download again. Remove them from the Models screen, or uninstall the App.

Uninstalling Nexus Chat also removes everything it stored on the phone.

Backups you made are yours and are not touched by the steps above. Delete them where you saved them: in Google Drive, open Settings > Manage apps in Drive on the web, find Nexus Chat and choose Delete hidden app data; for a folder backup, delete the .nxv files from that folder.

Request deletion of data held off your phone

Email privacy@nexussyntegra.com with the subject Delete my Nexus Chat data. Tell us which of the items below you want deleted. Because none of this data is tied to an account, include anything that helps us find it: for a report, the approximate date and time you sent it and the text of the reply you reported. We reply within 30 days.
